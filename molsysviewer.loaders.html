molsysviewer.loaders.array_native_molsys module#

Array-native serialization for materialized MolSys structural data.

This module deliberately stops before transport. It prepares a small JSON-compatible metadata payload plus contiguous numeric arrays that an AnyWidget adapter can send as binary buffers.
