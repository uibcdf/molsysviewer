# Presets

Presets are curated bundles of representations and style parameters.

Use presets when you want a sensible multi-representation setup with one call.

You can apply presets to:

- the whole structure: `view.whole.set_representation(preset=...)`
- a region: `region.set_representation(preset=...)`

Built-in presets
- `auto` (alias: `automatic`)
- `atomic-detail`
- `polymer-and-ligand`
- `polymer-cartoon`
- `coarse-surface`
- `empty`
