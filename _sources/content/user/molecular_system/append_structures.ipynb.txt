    "(User_MolecularSystem_AppendStructures)=\n",
    "# Appending structures\n",
    "\n",
    "This page documents `view.load(..., mode=\"append_structures\")`.\n",
    "\n",
    "Use this loading mode to append additional structures to the current molecular system. If your molecular system represents a trajectory, structures are often called \"frames\". Appending structures is the most common way to extend a trajectory with an additional chunk.\n",
    "\n",
    "`mode=\"append_structures\"` is loader sugar: it loads the additional structures and reconciles the viewer state. For MolSysMT-owned live editing, use `view.addons.molsysmt.basic.append_structures(...)` from the MolSysMT addon.\n",
    "\n",
    "For more details and additional examples, see the MolSysMT tutorial: [Append structures](https://www.uibcdf.org/molsysmt/content/user/tools/basic/append_structures).\n"
   ]
  },

    "\n",
