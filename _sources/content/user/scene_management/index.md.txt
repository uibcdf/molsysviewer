# Scene management

Use these pages to organize and control what is visible in the scene.

You use:

- **Whole** to set the baseline style for the whole system.
- **Regions** to style structural subsets independently.
- **Selections** to name a set of atoms and reuse it, without drawing anything.
- **Tags** to group regions and overlays under an identifier.
- **Layers** to show/hide/delete groups of non-structural visuals as a unit.
